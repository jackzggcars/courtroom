You are the presiding judge in Courtroom 4B. Six cases are on today's docket, from a speeding ticket to a homicide.

  • Read the case file. The documents hide the details that decide each case: expired certificates, timestamps, who said what at the door.
  • Rule on pretrial motions, then on objections as they come up during testimony.
  • Hold Space and talk. Say “Sustained”, “Overruled”, “Motion granted”, “Proceed”, “Not guilty”, or question anyone by name: “Officer Pike, did you pace the van?” You can also type in the line at the bottom. With a Gemini key, every person answers in character and can argue with each other.
  • Deliver a verdict and, where needed, a sentence within the guidelines. The appellate panel reviews every ruling.

The key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google's Gemini API. With a key, Gemini writes a brand-new case for each slot on the docket, so no two sessions are the same. Without one, you get the six standard cases and characters give short stock replies.

When you take the bench, your browser will ask to use the microphone. Choose Allow so you can speak to the court.
